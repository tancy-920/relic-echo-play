# Hub expansion asset provenance

SunnySide World V1 components: Daniel Diggle. Only selected tree/bush/rock/fence/statue/chest crops, six crop/crate sprites and the chimney-smoke strip are adopted from the user-provided local library. Crop/crate sprites and the smoke strip are byte-identical copies; tileset pieces are selected crops. Paths and source hashes are in source.json.

Official V1 terms previously checked on 2026-10-23: https://danieldiggle.itch.io/sunnyside and https://www.danieldiggle.com/sunnysideworld.php . V1 permits free and commercial game use and modification; attribution is appreciated but optional. Do not repackage or resell the source assets, including NFTs, or use them to train AI. This is not a CC0 declaration. This project uses these as runtime sprites, not training data.

The large tree, carved pillar, arch and wall reuse this project's previously adopted ruins-atlas.png. They are not SunnySide assets. Terrain, buildings, whale bones and the other original components identified in source.json are project-authored pixel construction. No ownership of the entire source library is asserted. Original library files and existing project art remain unchanged.
